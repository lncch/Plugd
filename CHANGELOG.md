# What's new in Plugd

Plugd shows the newest section in its update window and in "What's New" after an update.
Keep each line plain text, one change per "- " line.

## 0.3.7
- Your Mac no longer goes to sleep while FORScan is open, so a long write doesn't get cut off. Closing a MacBook's lid still sleeps it.
- Plugd warns you when the car battery is under 12.4 V, so you put a charger on before changing settings.
- Fixed "Adapter changed" showing when no adapter was plugged in.

## 0.3.6
- Plugd now runs on macOS 12 Monterey, so older Macs like a 2015 MacBook Air work too. On macOS 12 there's no menu bar icon or Open at login.

## 0.3.5
- Bluetooth adapters: pair one in System Settings and Plugd finds it. This is new and not tested with a real adapter yet, so tell me how it goes.
- If Plugd doesn't find your adapter, pick it in Settings > Adapter.
- Plugd now updates itself. When a new version is out, click Update and it installs and reopens.

## 0.3.4
- Notarized by Apple, so it opens like any other app. No more Open Anyway.

## 0.3.3
- Fixed setup freezing on Intel Macs.

## 0.3.2
- One download for Apple Silicon and Intel.
- New gradient icon.

## 0.3.1
- Live Dashboard: RPM, speed, temperatures and more while FORScan is closed.
- Menu bar icon, and a Settings page with Open at login.

## 0.3.0
- Welcome pages and a sidebar with Home, Engine codes, Files & backups and How to use.
