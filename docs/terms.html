What Plugd is

Plugd is a free, open source Mac app that sets up Wine and FORScan and connects FORScan to your OBD adapter. Plugd itself only reads from your car. Any changes to your car's modules are made by you, using FORScan.

Your car is your responsibility

Changing module settings can stop features from working, and in rare cases can leave a module unusable. Before you write anything with FORScan:

  • Back up your As-Built data and module settings.
  • Keep a battery charger on the car.
  • Only change settings you understand.
